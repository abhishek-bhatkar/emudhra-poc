Is your browser still exposed to the eMudhra weak-key attack?

In Oct 2026, eight eMudhra/emSign intermediate CA keys were so weak (one was literally 4) that anyone holding their public certificates could recover the private keys and forge trusted certificates. Browsers that received the Emergency update reject those chains. Frozen or unpatched browsers accept them. This page probes your browser against the incident's live test hosts. Your own browser is the oracle.
