TLS trust, tested from your browser

Did your browser make you immune to the weak-key CA attack?

In Oct 2026, eight eMudhra/emSign intermediate CA keys were so weak (one was literally 4) that anyone with their public certificates could recover the private keys and forge trusted certificates. Patched browsers reject those chains. Frozen or unpatched browsers accept them. This page probes your browser against the incident's live test hosts. Your browser is the oracle.
